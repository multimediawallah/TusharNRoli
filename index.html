Day One · Evening

Sangeet Sandhya

Step into after-dark glamour and elevated eveningwear. Ladies, think statement cocktail dresses, elegant evening gowns and sequined looks. Gentlemen, opt for sharply tailored blazers, polished shirts, velvet jackets or refined evening separates. Keep it polished, party-ready and finished with a touch of sparkle.

Dress for the grand celebration in opulent traditional attire. Ladies, bring out your richly embellished festive ensembles; gentlemen, arrive in elegant suits or classic occasionwear.
